_Effective: 28 September 2026_

Violin Clef ("the app") is a note-reading trainer; its developer is also called Violin
Clef. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: **everything stays on your
phone. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never
records or uploads your voice.**

## Microphone and your voice

The app asks for microphone access so it can hear you name the notes out loud during a
training session. Your voice is turned into note names (do, re, mi, …) **on your phone**
by a built-in speech recogniser. The audio is:

- processed only while a session runs, in the phone's memory;
- **never saved** to storage, and never recorded;
- **never sent anywhere**.

You can refuse or withdraw microphone access at any time in Android's settings. Without
it, training sessions can't hear your answers.

## What the app stores on your phone

To show your progress, the app saves on your phone:

- **Session results**: date and time, clef, level, scoring mode, tempo, accuracy,
  average reaction time and score. For each note: the note you were shown, whether your
  answer was correct, the kind of mistake (for example "wrong pitch" or "a little early"),
  and your reaction time. It does not store what you said, only the result.
- **Settings**: tempo, clef, level, scoring mode, recognition language, whether the
  metronome click is on, and whether you finished the introduction.

This data never leaves your phone through the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its
data in Android's settings, deletes it.

**Android backup.** If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include these
results and settings in your phone's backup to your Google account, like any other app's
data, so they can be restored on a new phone. The speech model is left out of backups. The
backup is handled by Android and Google under your Google account; the developer has no
access to it. You can turn backup off in Android's settings.

## Internet access: downloading the speech model

The first time you use a recognition language, the app downloads that language's speech
model (a file of about 45 MB) from the Vosk project's server at `alphacephei.com`. This
is the only network connection the app makes. Nothing about you is sent: the request only
asks for the model file. As with any download, that server can see your IP address; see
the Vosk project's own policies for how it handles server logs. The model is stored on
your phone and reused afterwards.

## Other permissions

- **Vibration**: used for the optional haptic metronome pulse.
- **Network state**: used to check for a connection before downloading the speech model.

## What the app does not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No advertising and no ad identifiers.
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking services.
- No selling or sharing of data with anyone. The developer never receives any of your
  data.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children,
because it does not send any data off the phone.
